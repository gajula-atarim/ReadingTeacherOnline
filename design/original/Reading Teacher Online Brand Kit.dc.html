01 — LOGO

An open book on a screen

The mark combines a monitor and an open book: real reading instruction, delivered online. Keep it whole. The icon may be used alone where the name already appears nearby, such as a social avatar or favicon.

READING TEACHER
ONLINE
WORDMARK TYPE

Josefin Sans Light for "Reading Teacher", Josefin Sans Bold with very wide tracking for "Online". Use this pairing only to echo the logo, for example in a website header or email footer, and never as a replacement for the logo file.

02 — COLOUR

Slate navy and teal

The core colours are taken from the graphics on readingteacheronline.com.au and match the teal in the logo. Light Teal is the accent, used for highlights and secondary calls to action. Soft tints colour-code topics and cards.

03 — TYPOGRAPHY

Type chosen for readers

Lexend was designed to improve reading fluency, with open letterforms and generous spacing. It carries all headings and body copy. Josefin Sans appears only in small uppercase labels, echoing the logo. Both are free on Google Fonts.

04 — IMAGERY

Real faces, real books

Show adults and children reading together in warm, natural light. Books and pages should be visible. Marilyn's portrait anchors the About page, newsletter and course material so families know who is teaching.

DO
Warm daylight and soft backgrounds
Books, letters and pages in frame
Genuine smiles and focus
Round image corners (16–24px)
AVOID
Cold office or classroom stock
Children alone on tablets
Heavy filters or dark overlays
Text placed over faces
05 — VOICE

An experienced teacher at the kitchen table

Write in the first person, as Marilyn. Speak to parents, grandparents and adult learners as capable partners. Plain words, short paragraphs, one clear next step.

01
Practical advice
Things a parent can try tonight, with a book already on the shelf.
02
Simple strategies
Explain phonics terms the first time they appear. No jargon left unexplained.
03
Real progress
Celebrate small wins. Encourage without overpromising.

The kit in use

Buttons, a website hero, a newsletter signup and a product card, built only from the colours and type above.
